ARS SPRINT V31 PRO

Sprint evaluation PWA focused on 5 m / 10 m / 20 m, inspired by the simple sideline workflow of Metric Sprint.

V31 fixes the automatic-start state machine, waits for video capture finalization before saving, uses robust frame-level velocity sampling, rejects extreme one-frame velocity spikes, reports peak velocity location, and stores the velocity profile for the result curve.

Scope: 5/10/20 m only. No 5-0-5, 5-10-5, 30 m or 40 m.

Important: this build is a prototype and has not been validated against photocells/timing gates with a real athlete session.
